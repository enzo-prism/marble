Marble 2.7 improves how typed and pasted workouts are read and reviewed before saving. It also adds exercise details to the Shortcuts app.

ACCESS AND PRIVACY
No account, sign-in, subscription, purchase, or review credentials are required. Marble supports iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 26.0 or later. Core training data is stored locally, with no advertising, third-party tracking, or Marble-operated server. There is no Marble Watch app; Apple Watch and Garmin workouts are imported through Apple Health. Strava is not configured and its UI is hidden.

WORKOUT REVIEW
1. Open Add, the default tab. Paste:
Straight Leg Speed Bounds (2 sets)
Sprints, 2 sets, 50m each
Bench Press 3x8 @ 60 kg
Tap Review Workout. Review lists the three exercises in the order written: two bounds sets with no reps or weight, two 50 m sprint sets, and three bench sets. Values can be edited before saving. Original text shows what was entered.
2. Add a line Marble cannot organize, such as "round 2 of 3 felt easy". Save stays disabled until that line is fixed with Edit Text or kept with "Keep in workout notes". Nothing is dropped silently.
3. Save, then open Log > Workout History, open the session, and choose Repeat Workout. Repeat opens a review with the same exercises and sets in the same order. Opening Repeat alone does not save anything.
4. In Settings > Units, choose kg. A weight entered without a unit, such as "Bench Press 3x8 @ 60", is reviewed in kg.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE
Parsing is on-device. Apple Intelligence is used only when available for the device and language. Ordinary gym notation and Hevy/Strong CSV imports have non-model parsing paths and do not need it. Text written in sentences may show a reading stage with a Stop button, which returns to editable text. Dates, titles, and numbers from a model reading are checked against the entered text, and review identifies readings made with Apple Intelligence. Model output is never saved without the user's review.

SHORTCUTS AND SIRI
In the Shortcuts app, Marble exercise entities now include Favorite, Last Performed, Total Sets, Heaviest Set, Most Reps, and Usual Range, computed on device from the user's logged sets. The existing actions (Log a Set of an Exercise, start or finish a workout, rest timer controls) are unchanged.

PROGRESS AND OPTIONAL FEATURES
Progress shows Top Exercises across the whole history, with each exercise's heaviest set and its date, plus attributed quotes. Details holds charts, Daily Highlights, and Monthly Reports when enough history exists. Daily Highlights appears only within its configured daily window (Settings > Daily Highlights). Quotes are attributed text, not athlete endorsements.

Apple Health is optional and permission-based. It can read workouts and body measurements and optionally write completed strength sessions. Core logging can be reviewed without granting Health access.

BACKUPS AND STORAGE
Settings > Data & Backups exports and restores a JSON backup through Files; please use disposable test data for restore checks. JSON backups exclude progress photos and videos. If persistent storage cannot open, logging pauses and a recovery screen offers retry and a user-initiated recovery copy through the share sheet; nothing is uploaded automatically.

There is no data model change from 2.6.1. Screenshots use sample training data.
