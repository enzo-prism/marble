Marble 2.7 (87) replaces the previously approved, unreleased build 86. Please review build 87, which adds the Apple Watch companion, Share extension, optional native iPhone Health workouts, dictation, expanded App Intents and clearer Progress Details.

No account or sign-in is required, and there are no review credentials. Core training records stay on the device. There are no ads, third-party tracking or Marble-operated servers. Exported JSON backups exclude actual progress photos and videos.

REVIEW PATH
1. Open Add, the default tab. Paste:
Push day
Bench Press 3x8 @ 185 lb
Tap Review Workout, inspect the structured sets, edit a value or note, then save. Apple Intelligence is used when available; ordinary notation also has a deterministic on-device parser. Parsing is editable review before insertion.
2. Before saving another review, leave and relaunch. Choose Resume Workout and confirm your edits remain. Resume does not save a workout. Discard Draft starts over.
3. In Log, open Workout History, search for Bench Press and open the saved workout. Repeat Workout opens a new editable review; canceling does not insert a session. Share prepares that workout's text summary.
4. Open Progress, then Details. Compare the colored metric cues, weekly volume and category charts. Exact values, labels, symbols and Audio Graphs supplement color. Check light/dark and larger text.
5. Start a workout from Add or the Marble Shortcuts action. Log a set, run rest, pause/resume if desired and finish. The active session and rest timer appear together in a Live Activity when permitted. Ordinary logging works without Health access.
6. Dictate Workout on a compatible device with local speech assets and microphone permission. Stop, edit the transcript and review before saving. From Notes, Files or Photos, share supported workout text/files/images to Marble. Shared content is stored in a bounded access-group keychain inbox; accepting a review is required before sets are committed.
7. With a paired Apple Watch and Marble open on the iPhone, open Marble on Watch. Start, repeat the phone's last confirmed set, manage rest and finish. This Watch app is a phone-connected controller; it does not independently collect wrist heart rate or run a Watch HealthKit workout.
8. Optionally enable Settings > Apple Health > Track live strength workouts in Apple Health, grant permission, then start/pause/resume/finish. The iPhone session uses strength training with an unspecified location. Heart rate requires an available compatible sensor. Historical exports use Other for unclassified activity and do not infer session effort from set RPE.
9. In Apple's Shortcuts app, find Marble's public actions for logging sets, managing sessions, finding/opening workouts and preparing summaries. Private workout results require authentication. These use regular App Intents APIs; custom Siri AI 27.3 developer-preview resources are excluded from this public build.

Strava remains unconfigured and hidden. Apple Health and system surfaces are optional. iPhone and iPad require iOS/iPadOS 26.0 or later; Apple Intelligence and local dictation depend on device, language and system availability.
