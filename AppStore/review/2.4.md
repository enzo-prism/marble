# Marble 2.4 review notes

Marble does not require an account or sign-in. All core workout, supplement,
planning, trend, bodyweight, and backup data is stored locally. The app has no
ads, third-party tracking, or Marble-operated server.

Apple Health access is optional. Marble reads workouts and body measurements
only after permission is granted, and can optionally write completed strength
sessions to Apple Health. Garmin workouts enter through Apple Health. Strava is
not configured in this build and its UI remains hidden. No credentials are
required for review.

The app may show Apple's standard review prompt after a finished workout with
sets, the first imported workout saved, or a genuine personal record. There is
no custom rate-us screen. The prompt is skipped during UI testing and never
appears on cold launch.

## What is new

- **Add / Log / Progress** tab bar. Add is the default AI-first workout composer;
  Calendar and Supplements are Log modes.
- **Paste or Type** workout entry for natural-language prose, common gym notation,
  Hevy, Strong, and Notes-style workouts, including
  a week of dated notes or a CSV export. Parsing stays on-device; the user
  reviews every set before save. Apple Intelligence structures prose when it is
  available, while deterministic parsing handles notation and fallback. Hevy warmup
  sets are skipped; failed 0-rep
  sets are kept; RPE, notes, session length, and library matching are visible
  in review. Close name matches do not silently merge into an existing exercise.
  Numbered `Day 1` / `Session 2` labels become workout titles, not exercises.
- Scan a multi-page handwritten notebook; several dated days hand off to Paste
  or Type. A single page still reviews in Scan, with the same library matching.
- Sprint V6: tenths-precision timing, an in-log stopwatch, and multiple sprint
  plans per exercise.
- Editable and deletable weigh-ins.
- Deployment target iOS 26.0.
- Reliability fixes across backup restore, number entry, and system controls.

## Suggested review path

1. Open **Add**. Paste or type `Push day` followed by `Bench Press 3x8 @ 185 lb`,
   tap **Review Workout**, inspect the structured sets, and save. The same screen
   accepts `.txt`, Hevy CSV, and Strong CSV files. Nothing is saved before review.
2. From Add, open **Plan**, start a workout, and use the active-workout accessory
   above the tab bar to reopen the session and add or complete sets.
3. Open **Log**, tap **+**, choose an exercise, and log a set. The logging
   screen shows the last set and personal-record target. Switch Log modes
   (Sets / Calendar / Supplements) with the segmented control.
4. Open **Progress** to review Weekly Goal, strength coaching, Daily Highlights,
   and bodyweight. Edit a weigh-in from Progress or Settings > Body.
5. Open a sprint exercise and log a rep with tenths timing or the stopwatch.
6. Open **Add > Settings** to review units, weekly goal, reminders, Apple
   Health options, privacy information, and **Data & Backups**.
7. Add Marble's **Weekly Goal** widget. The medium Home Screen widget includes
   a **Log set** button; the Lock Screen supports circular, rectangular, and
   inline families.

The screenshot dataset is deterministic and contains sample training data only.

## Accessibility and device scope

Marble supports iPhone and iPad; there is no Apple Watch app in this release.
Apple Watch and Garmin workouts can enter Marble through Apple Health on the
paired iPhone. The Weekly Goal widget is an iPhone/iPad widget and should not be
interpreted as an Apple Watch companion.

The interface uses Dynamic Type, light/dark palettes, labeled standard controls,
and text/icon cues in addition to color. Progress charts include VoiceOver Audio
Graph descriptors. Accessibility Nutrition Labels are maintained separately from
the review submission and must not be published until the release candidate has
passed the complete physical-device accessibility matrix on both iPhone and iPad.
