Marble is a local workout journal for iPhone and iPad. No account, credentials, ads, third-party tracking, or Marble-operated server are required. Apple Health is optional. There is no Marble Apple Watch app; Apple Watch workouts can be imported through Apple Health. Strava is not configured in this release and its UI is hidden.

This update adds searchable completed-session history, repeat-as-review drafts, fuller interrupted-review recovery, and a simpler Progress overview with dated weight personal records and training quotes. It also improves entry responsiveness, readable history layouts, and storage/backup failure handling.

Suggested review:
1. In Add, enter “Bench Press 3x8 @ 185 lb” and tap Review Workout. Edit a set or note. Parsing is on-device; Apple Intelligence is used when available, with deterministic notation parsing and fallback.
2. Relaunch before saving and choose Resume Workout. Confirm the saved edits and save the workout once.
3. Open Log > Workout History (titled Workout Sessions). Search for Bench Press or filter by date, open the session, and choose Repeat Workout. Review/edit the draft; opening Repeat alone does not save it.
4. Open Progress. Top Exercises shows qualifying weighted exercises with a personal-record weight and its date. Tap or swipe the quote footer. Open Details for goals, charts, records, reports, and body measurements.
5. In Add, open More (ellipsis) > Settings > Data & Backups for JSON export/restore. Photo and video files are excluded from JSON backups. The workout-shaped Add toolbar button starts or opens a workout; Workout Plan is under More.

The Unable to open workouts screen is only for a storage-opening failure, not normal onboarding. It preserves original files, offers Try Again and explicit recovery-copy export, and does not accept new logging into temporary storage. Recovery-copy export does not itself repair a database or send data to support.

Session history contains finished workouts and pasted/scanned sessions. Standalone sets and Apple Health imported summaries remain in Log. Repeating a sprint workout measured in tenths requests confirmation before creating rounded whole-second draft times, retaining exact previous values in notes and leaving the original unchanged.
