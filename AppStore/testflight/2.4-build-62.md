default Progress tab shows only a weekly status and Details action with generous
white space. Open Details and verify Summary, Training, Body & Habits, and

## Focused acceptance pass

- Overview: the default Progress tab is quiet and uncluttered, with one readable
  weekly status, one clear Details action, and no duplicated metrics or controls.
- Details: weekly goal, daily highlights, consistency and volume, exercise
  filters, reports, body metrics, habits, supplements, and milestones remain
  organized and discoverable.
- Adaptivity: content remains usable in light and dark appearance, maximum
  Larger Text, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast, iPhone SE, regular
  iPhone, and iPad portrait and landscape.
- Regression: Add, Log, imports, workout sessions, rest timer, widgets, Settings,
  backup and restore, HealthKit boundaries, and shipped-data migration still work.
