## What to Test (en-US)

Marble 2.4 build 61 polishes the AI-first Add experience across light and dark
appearance. Paste or type a workout, clear or review it, and verify Plan and
Settings in More. Recheck the full-month Calendar, grayscale Trends, Add Set
Save and Save + Next, and progress-photo crop editing. Test maximum Larger Text,
VoiceOver, Increase Contrast, Reduce Transparency, and iPad layouts. Send the
smallest workout text, screen, appearance, and text-size setting that reproduces
any issue.

## Focused acceptance pass

- Add: the editor is the first task; placeholder text is concise; Clear has a
  reachable 44-point target; Review Workout stays visible; Start or open workout
  remains persistent; Plan and Settings are discoverable in More.
- Parsing and review: prose, `3x8 @ 185 lb`, timed and bodyweight movements,
  Notes, Hevy/Strong CSV, unresolved-line retry, draft recovery, Shortcut
  handoff, exact exercise identity, and deterministic fallback.
- Add Set: one toolbar Save action closes the logger, while the primary bottom
  Save + Next action keeps it open and preserves the expected next-set values.
- Calendar and media: the entire month is visible without unbounded scrolling;
  month controls meet the minimum hit target; progress photo Reset and Save do
  not crash or disappear.
- Trends: status and series remain understandable without color, including
  tooltip states, in light, dark, and Increase Contrast appearances.
- Accessibility: maximum Larger Text, VoiceOver names and actions, 44-point
  targets, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, and non-color cues.
- iPad: Add, Calendar, and Trends use readable centered widths in portrait and
  landscape without stretched content, clipping, or cropped controls.
- Regression: workout sessions, rest timer, Log modes, imports, widgets,
  backup/restore, HealthKit boundaries, and shipped-2.3 migration.

## Exit criteria

- Full unit, UI, automated accessibility, snapshot, and project audit gates pass.
- Light/dark and iPhone/iPad visual baselines are reviewed and committed.
- The signed build is uploaded from exact merged `main`, reaches `VALID`, and
  strict TestFlight validation reports no blocker.
- Internal `test group A` receives it through all-build access; do not add it
  manually or trigger external Beta App Review.
- App Store 2.4 uses manual release. TestFlight upload, internal distribution,
  review submission, or approval must never be described as public release.
