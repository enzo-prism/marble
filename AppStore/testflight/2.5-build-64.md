# Marble 2.5 (64) — What to test

- Review a pasted workout, correct weights and dates, then close and relaunch Marble.
  Resume should preserve corrections, selected sessions, and exercise matches. Discard
  should remove only the draft. Nothing is logged until you confirm.
- Open Workout History from Log. Search by workout or exercise, filter by date, and
  open a completed session. Repeat should open a fresh review without adding duplicates.
- Check sprint session details retain exact recorded tenths. Repeating a sprint warns
  that editable duration uses whole seconds and keeps the precise prior result in notes.
- Check Progress still opens to the quiet overview and Details remains one tap away.
- On an ordinary upgrade, existing workouts must remain intact. If storage cannot open,
  the app now preserves the original files and offers Retry or a private recovery copy.
  Do not intentionally damage your real workout database to test this state.

No SwiftData schema change (V6). Device-only Health, Siri, widget, lock-screen, and
performance checks remain part of physical-device acceptance before public release.
