# Marble 2.4 (57) TestFlight plan

## What to Test (en-US)

Marble 2.4 adds bulk workout import. Please try a real Hevy or Strong CSV, or
paste a multi-day workout from Notes. Review every exercise and set before
saving, especially warmups, failed sets, RPE, notes, rest, dates, units,
supersets, weak exercise matches, and newly created exercises. Also check that
Train, Log, Progress, sprints, and weigh-ins still work normally. Send feedback
with the source format and the smallest sample that reproduces any mismatch.

## Focused acceptance pass

- Hevy: warmups, failure rows, RPE, notes, session timing, and repeated exercise
  blocks.
- Strong: semicolon-delimited exports, decimal commas, kg, distance, equipment
  suffixes, and `superset_id` notes.
- Notes: `Day` / `Session` boundaries, `RPE 8` / `@RPE 8`, multiple workouts,
  weak matches, and new-exercise creation.
- Scan: multi-page order, exercise reordering, visible rest/RPE/notes, and
  re-scan deduplication.
- Regression: Train / Log / Progress navigation, planned-set completion, sprint
  timing, editable weigh-ins, backup export, and restore preview.

## Exit criteria

- At least 48 hours installed by more than the owner account.
- At least one complete Hevy, Strong, Notes, and scan pass with no silent loss.
- No launch, save, migration, or restore crash.
- Any mismatch is either fixed or documented accurately in App Store metadata.
