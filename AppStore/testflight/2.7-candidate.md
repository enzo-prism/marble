Marble 2.7 is mainly a workout-import update. Add keeps what your note actually says, and nothing is saved until you have reviewed it.

Please try these (1–5 in Add, by paste or typing):

1. This Apple Notes workout, blank lines included:

   9/4/26

   Straight Leg Speed Bounds (2 sets)

   Knee Drive Speed Bounds (2 sets)

   Resistance Rope Sprint 2 sets , 50m each

   Sprints , 2 sets , 50m each

   Review should show September 4, 2026, four exercises in that order and eight sets. Bounds have no reps or weight; both sprints show 50 m per set. Save, then open Workout History and Repeat: the order and all eight sets must match.

2. A note with a line Marble can't organize, such as "Bench Press 3x8 @ 185" followed by "round 2 of 3 felt easy". Save stays disabled until you edit and retry the line or keep it in workout notes.

3. A workout written in plain sentences, with Apple Intelligence on. Check that dates, titles and numbers come only from your text, that weights without a unit follow your kg or lb setting, and that Stop returns you to your text.

4. "Bench 225x5x3" should come back as 3 sets of 5 at 225, or stay in review for you to fix; never 225 sets. A clean gym-notation workout like "Squat 5x5 @ 225" should reach review almost instantly.

5. With VoiceOver, a set with no numbers reads as "Set recorded".

6. In Progress, Top Exercises should list your most-logged exercises across your whole history, whatever range Details is set to. Bests from an earlier year show the year.

No data model change. Existing workouts, drafts and backups should be unaffected; please report anything that looks different after updating from 2.6 or 2.6.1.
